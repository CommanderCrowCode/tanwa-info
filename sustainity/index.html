The four prompts

These are the exact prompts from the session. Copy them into ChatGPT or Claude on your own workshop data.

Prompt 2 uses second-class-order-lines.csv. All other prompts use orders-one-row-per-order.csv, with the mapping line.

1. Detect
"An order is late when Days for shipping (real) is more than Days for shipment (scheduled), and Delivery Status is not Shipping canceled. What share of orders is late for each Shipping Mode? Count each Order Id once."
2. Understand
"For Second Class, add up Order Profit Per Order over the lines of each late order. What is the total? Check it on order 50554."
3. Compare
"Compare: upgrade Second Class to First Class at $92 an order (it keeps its 2 day promise), or promise First Class 2 days and Second Class 4 days. Assume a late order costs 10% of its Order Item Total, added up over its lines. First, show the credit on all late orders over 3 years, before any fix."
4. Escalate
"Write a five line escalation note to our commercial head: the signal, who is affected, the fixes with net cost, our recommendation, and what we need approved, by when."

Use these only on workshop data, or in your own company's approved tools. Never company data in a personal account.

What we demo live

Four short steps, about one tool each. You do not need any of these tools today.

Step 1, Claude chat

"This is a sample of 500 orders from a supply chain dataset. In five short bullets: what is in this file, and which three questions about delivery risk could it answer?"

Step 2, Claude Cowork

"In this folder, open weekly.csv. Make a line chart of late_share_pct by week_start, with dashed lines at the lowest and the highest of the earlier weeks. Mark the last row, a what if week we planted. Save it as weekly-watch.xlsx."

Step 3, Claude Code

"Read orders.csv. Write a short Python script that, for Second Class orders, adds up profit on late orders by region and shows the top five. Late means days_actual is greater than days_promised and delivery_status is not Shipping canceled. Run it, then show me the table and the script."

Step 4, three agents on a relay

"Check weekly.csv. Is the last week's late share outside the normal range? If it is, send impact-analyst a signal."

Step 4 shows three AI agents passing a finding to each other, ending in a note for a person to approve. No agent approves anything on its own.
